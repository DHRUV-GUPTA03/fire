Product Overview

Intelligent Mechanical Protection for Every Indian Kitchen

GasSafe is a dedicated, fully mechanical emergency safety attachment that bridges between your domestic LPG cylinder and normal regulator, delivering foolproof fail-safe protection.

Abnormal Gas Flow Detection

The device continuously monitors inline pressure dynamics. If an abnormal surge occurs due to a disconnected pipe, ruptured rubber hose, or burner defect, the device senses the sudden pressure change instantly.

Instant Automatic Shut-Off

In the event of a major leak or catastrophic line break, the internal spring-loaded valve snaps tightly shut in less than half a second, choking off gas flow at the cylinder source before an explosive mixture can form.

100% Mechanical Operation

Designed to work entirely without electricity, batteries, wiring, or software. Its mechanical diaphragm and spring system operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year without relying on external power or charging.

Universal LPG Compatibility

Precision engineered to fit standard domestic 14.2 kg LPG cylinders from all major Indian oil marketing companies, including Indane (IOCL), Bharat Gas (BPCL), and HP Gas (HPCL).

Easy 30-Second DIY Installation

No technician, plumber, or special tools needed. Simply fit GasSafe onto the cylinder valve, hand-tighten the collar, and snap your existing regulator on top. Transferable to new cylinders in seconds.

Built-in Leak Test & Gauge

The integrated pressure gauge provides clear visual feedback on cylinder gas reserves and allows home cooks to perform simple zero-gas leak tests before lighting the stove.

Working Principle

How GasSafe Protects Your Home

A seamless 3-stage mechanical safeguard that prevents gas accumulation and catastrophic kitchen fire disasters.

01 Normal Operation

Normal Regulated Gas Flow

During everyday cooking, pressurized gas from the cylinder passes smoothly through the calibrated internal chambers of the GasSafe device directly into your standard pressure regulator and stove. The dial shows a steady green operating reading.

Constant 20–30% pressure optimization
02 Hazard Detection

Abnormal Flow Detection

If an emergency occurs — such as a hose slipping off, rubber tube rupture, rat-bite puncture, or burner valve failure — the rapid downstream pressure drop immediately unbalances the calibrated internal mechanical diaphragm.

Reacts to pressure differentials instantly
03 Instant Cut-Off

Instant Gas Supply Shut-Off

The precision spring-loaded shut-off piston instantly slams forward, locking against the valve seat within milliseconds. The entire gas flow from the cylinder is sealed right at the source, preventing kitchen gas buildup and fire explosion.

Sealed until manually reset after inspection
